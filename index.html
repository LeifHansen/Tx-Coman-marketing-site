FAQ

Hemp co-packing questions

What is hemp co-packing?

Co-packing (co-manufacturing) is when a licensed facility formulates, infuses, fills, packages and lab-tests your hemp-derived THC or CBD product under your brand. You own the brand and recipe. We run production, quality control and compliance.

Do you make marijuana or medical cannabis products?

No. We are a hemp-only facility. Every product we make is hemp-derived and stays within federal hemp limits for delta-9 THC. We do not produce recreational or medical cannabis products.

What are your minimum order quantities?

Our minimums are among the lowest in Texas. Beverage runs start around 1,000 units, and gummies and capsules start around 500 units, so new brands can launch and test the market without a large upfront commitment. Tell us your target volume and we will quote it.

What formats do you produce?

Aluminum cans, bottles, 750 ml bottles, gummies and capsules, all produced in Texas with full-panel lab testing. See beverages, gummies and capsules.

Do you co-pack kava, kratom or ashwagandha products?

Yes. Alongside hemp-derived THC and CBD we co-pack kava, kratom, ashwagandha, functional mushrooms and other botanicals in drinks, gummies and capsules, with labeling that follows Texas and federal rules for each ingredient. Learn more about functional co-packing.

How long does a first production run take?

Typical timeline from signed agreement to finished product is 4 to 8 weeks, depending on formulation work, packaging lead times and testing. Reorders run in 2 to 3 weeks.

Can you help with formulation if I only have an idea?

Yes. Many brands come to us with a concept and a target dose. We develop flavor, base and shelf-life, then produce bench samples for approval before any production run.

Can you ship outside Texas?

Yes. Hemp-derived products ship to every state where they are permitted. We review your label for multistate distribution and provide COAs with QR codes for each batch.
