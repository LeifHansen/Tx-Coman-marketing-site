Why partner selection matters

Manufacturing isn’t one-size-fits-all. Neither is your brand.

The right manufacturing relationship starts with specifics. A facility that’s great at high-volume cans may not be the right fit for a small bottled pilot.

A mismatch rarely shows up on the first quote. It shows up later as reformulation, packaging that doesn’t run on the line, or a pilot that can’t scale. We connect qualified inquiries with potential Texas manufacturing partners, so your first conversation can be about the actual project, not whether a plant runs your category.

Tell us what you need
  1. 01

    Format & line

    A slim-can line, a 750 mL glass filler and a gummy depositor are different equipment, crews and minimums.

  2. 02

    Formula readiness

    Idea, bench sample or production-ready recipe decides whether you need development, scale-up or filling only.

  3. 03

    Ingredient system

    Hemp-derived ingredients, emulsions and botanicals are accepted and handled differently from plant to plant.

  4. 04

    Run size & growth

    A plant built for long runs may not prioritize a pilot; a small-batch partner may cap out as you scale.

  5. 05

    Scope & responsibility

    Who sources cans and ingredients, coordinates testing, packs out and ships changes the real cost.

Questions we hear

Practical answers. Less guesswork.

We keep production answers specific to the project and avoid blanket claims about every partner.

Ask about your project
Are you a beverage manufacturing facility?

No. Beverage Co-Packing Texas is an independent project inquiry and manufacturing introduction service that connects qualified brands with potential Texas manufacturing partners.

Who is this service for?

Brands, founders and product teams planning commercial production of hemp-derived THC or CBD beverages, functional drinks or gummies. Inquiries are business-to-business only; we do not sell products to consumers.

Can you help with hemp THC and CBD beverage production?

Yes. We accept project inquiries for hemp-derived THC and CBD beverage concepts. Specific product acceptance, capabilities and requirements are evaluated by each partner.

Do you handle cans, bottles and gummies?

Those are our primary focus areas, including aluminum cans, glass and PET bottles, 750 mL bottles and gummies. Include your format, estimated volume and development stage in the project brief.

What are the minimum order quantities?

Minimums vary significantly by formula, packaging, line and manufacturing partner. Share your target run size and we can discuss realistic fit.

Can you help if I haven’t finalized my formula?

Yes. Describe your concept and your current development status so we can assess whether formulation or scale-up assistance is part of the conversation.

Should I share my formula in the inquiry?

No. Share only what you’re comfortable with at the inquiry stage. Proprietary formulas and detailed technical documents should be exchanged with a selected partner under an appropriate agreement.

What about documentation and regulations?

Requirements depend on the individual product and intended markets. Partners and qualified advisers should review ingredients, labeling, testing and other requirements before production.
