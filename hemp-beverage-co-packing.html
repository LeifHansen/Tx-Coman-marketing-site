Hemp-derived THC beverages bring a different production brief to the table than a conventional soft drink. Ingredient selection, emulsion behavior, sensory experience, packaging, quality requirements and batch planning all need to be discussed early. Our role is to help route your project to a relevant manufacturing conversation rather than make you cold-call facilities that may not handle your format.

Bottled beverages

Product development

Review of an existing formula or a discussion about creating one with the right specialists.

Production planning

A realistic conversation around volumes, packaging components, sourcing responsibilities and schedule.

A production partner that fits the product

A canned sparkling seltzer and a shelf-stable bottled mixer may require very different equipment, production steps and packaging suppliers. Tell us your base liquid, carbonation preference, package size, flavor concept and desired run size. We can help narrow the conversation to partners relevant to your project.

What to have ready before an introduction

Share whether you have a bench formula, commercial-ready formula or just a concept; the intended cannabinoid ingredients; the desired package; expected production volume; approximate timeline; and any packaging already purchased. A complete brief makes it easier to have a productive conversation with a co-manufacturer.

From pilot concept to repeat production

Early brands often need help turning a product idea into a manufacturable specification. Established brands may simply need a different canning line, bottle format or more capacity. We help surface the practical requirements so facilities can determine fit and scope.

Quality and documentation conversations

Exact ingredient, testing, labeling, and market requirements depend on the product and destination. These details should be reviewed with the selected manufacturing partner and appropriate advisers before production is agreed.

Common questions

Do you manufacture hemp THC beverages yourselves?

We coordinate introductions and inquiries with Texas manufacturing partners; we are not presenting the site as a single operating plant.

Can you help with both cans and bottles?

Yes. Share your preferred format and we can determine which relevant capabilities to discuss with potential partners.

What is the minimum production run?

Minimum quantities vary by line, format, formula and packaging. We request your target volume and discuss practical fit with partners.

Can I bring my own emulsion or formula?

Mention your existing ingredients or formula in the brief. The manufacturing partner will confirm compatibility and any required development work.
