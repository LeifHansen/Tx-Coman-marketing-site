Why hemp drinks need a specialist co-packer

Cannabinoids are oil-soluble, so a drink needs a stable emulsion that stays homogeneous in the can, survives carbonation and heat, and still tests within label claims months later. A general beverage co-packer rarely has that process. We do, and we pair it with the lab testing and batch records retailers and distributors expect.
