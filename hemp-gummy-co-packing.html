Start with the piece and the package

A useful gummy inquiry specifies intended piece weight, gummies per package, serving concept, count configuration, preferred packaging and target annual volume. If custom tooling is involved, surface that as early as possible.

Gummy co-packing versus custom manufacturing

Filling branded pouches with existing gummies is different from developing and producing a custom infused gummy. Clearly describe whether you already have a formula, supplier and packaging or need a partner to scope the broader job.

Ingredients and quality discussions

Ingredient compatibility, homogeneity, testing plans, packaging and destination-market requirements should be worked through with the selected partner. This site does not certify a formula, make market-access representations or substitute for professional review.
