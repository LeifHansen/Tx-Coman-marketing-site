Packaging components

Glass or PET depends on the brief

Glass can create a premium shelf impression, while PET may offer weight and handling advantages. Either format has tradeoffs in barrier performance, process compatibility, supply and freight. The selected partner should confirm what the line can run.

What the bottler needs before quoting

Provide bottle capacity and shape if known, closure style, liquid characteristics, target unit volume, labeling plan, finished-goods configuration and your desired launch window. If your bottle is already sourced, share its technical drawing.

750 mL and multi-serve concepts

Premium mixers and shareable beverages often have different shelf presentation and fulfillment needs from single-serve cans. Discuss closures, neck finish, final case dimensions and retail presentation early.

Don't treat filling as the only stage

Formula readiness, ingredient staging, packaging procurement, quality requirements, storage and freight all affect a bottling project. We help organize those questions before an introduction.
