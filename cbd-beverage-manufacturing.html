Why CBD beverages require a complete manufacturing brief

A formula that tastes great in a small sample may behave differently when batched, packaged or stored. A productive inquiry includes the carrier, flavor profile, target serving, intended shelf life, ingredient sourcing plan and packaging specification.

Cans or bottles? Start with distribution

Cans can be attractive for ready-to-drink refreshment and multipacks; bottles can support concentrate, shot or premium mixer presentations. Consider shipping weight, merchandising, filling-line compatibility and component procurement before choosing. Our cans vs. bottles guide walks through the tradeoffs.
