Why CBD beverages require a complete manufacturing brief

A formula that tastes great in a small sample may behave differently when batched, packaged or stored. A productive inquiry includes the carrier, flavor profile, target serving, intended shelf life, ingredient sourcing plan and packaging specification.

Cans or bottles? Start with distribution

Cans can be attractive for ready-to-drink refreshment and multipacks; bottles can support concentrate, shot or premium mixer presentations. Consider shipping weight, merchandising, filling-line compatibility and component procurement before choosing.

Work with existing formulas or early-stage concepts

Have a formula? Include its stage of readiness. Just have a product idea? Describe the sensory direction and product positioning. We can discuss where formulation support may be required and identify the right type of manufacturing relationship.

Plan the business side

Lead times, minimums, available line formats and quotations are project-specific. Sharing your target volumes and release goals helps move discussions toward actionable proposals instead of generic price estimates.
