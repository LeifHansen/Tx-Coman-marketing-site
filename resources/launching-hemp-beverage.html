Planning Your First Hemp Beverage Production Run

A production-ready brief makes every manufacturer conversation faster and more useful.

  • Commercial planning resource
  • 3 min read
  • Published November 18, 2026
03.

A new hemp-derived THC or CBD beverage launch involves multiple workstreams. The formula, ingredients, package, production volume, vendor responsibilities and destination markets all influence the commercial plan. The following steps help organize the discussion without relying on invented manufacturing promises.

Step 1: Define the product experience

Write a concise description of flavor, sweetness, carbonation, package, intended serving and intended audience. If you have a reference beverage, share what you want to borrow from its sensory experience without assuming its formula is transferable.

Step 2: Clarify formula readiness

State whether the product is an idea, a bench sample, a pilot formula or a commercially produced recipe. Include who owns the technical documents and whether any ingredient or delivery systems are already sourced.

Step 3: Decide which packaging questions are open

For cans, describe preferred size and decoration. For bottles, include material, capacity, neck finish and closure if known. Think through cases, multipacks, labels and storage needs.

Step 4: Give a real volume range

An initial volume estimate can be a range rather than a firm commitment. Include a rough annual forecast if available. A facility can then assess whether the commercial scale aligns with its operation.

Step 5: Map what the facility will own

Ask whether the proposed scope includes sourcing, batching, filling, testing coordination, packing and freight. Avoid treating all those services as automatically included under ‘co-packing.’

Step 6: Review requirements before production

Ingredients, labels, documentation and intended markets require product-specific evaluation. The manufacturing partner and relevant professional advisers should address these requirements before a project is approved.

Step 7: Convert the brief into a partner conversation

Once you can clearly communicate the product, stage, package, volume and target schedule, you are ready for a much more productive introduction to a potential co-manufacturer.

Quick project checklist

Use it in your brief
  • Brand and company contact
  • Product and intended serving description
  • Cannabinoid / ingredient concept
  • Formula status and ownership
  • Can or bottle specifications
  • Target run volume and annual estimate
  • Primary distribution or destination markets
  • Packaging procurement status
  • Target schedule and budget expectations
