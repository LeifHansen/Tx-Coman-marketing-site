# Beverage Co-Packing Texas — brand & marketing guide
**Official domain:** https://beveragecopackingtx.com
**Positioning:** Independent Texas manufacturing introductions and project inquiries for business-to-business hemp THC, CBD, and functional ready-to-drink beverage brands; also gummies. Not a single operating co-packing plant.
**Primary audience:** beverage entrepreneurs, established beverage brands, cannabis/hemp beverage teams, formulators, product developers and procurement managers.

## Core message
**Your beverage. The right production partner.**
Supporting: “Cans. Bottles. Gummies. Built around your brief.”
CTA: “Find a manufacturing partner” / “Submit your production brief” / “Discuss your project.”

## Voice
Knowledgeable, B2B, concise, practical, packaging-aware, approachable. Talk about production planning and fit. Avoid cannabis-leaf visuals, wellness miracle claims, stoner stereotypes, or guarantees of specific factory capabilities.

Subject: Your Texas beverage manufacturing inquiry
“Thanks for sharing your production brief with Beverage Co-Packing Texas. To help identify potential partner fit, can you confirm your intended package format, whether the formula is production-ready, your expected first run size, and any target production window? We’ll use that information to frame a useful manufacturing discussion. Please avoid emailing confidential formula details before an appropriate agreement is in place.”

## Compliance guardrails
Do not assert specific THC legality, licensing, registration, shipment eligibility, safety certification, lab results or permitted ingredients. Do not assert universal MOQs, blanket turnarounds, exact shelf life, partner production capacities or guaranteed approval. Product-specific reviews are for individual manufacturers and advisers. No health/medical promises.
