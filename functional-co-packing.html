Functional drinks may combine botanical ingredients, flavors and other specialty components. Those combinations can affect formulation, sensory quality, ingredient handling and the right production process. We help structure a discussion with relevant manufacturing partners without promising that any particular ingredient or product category is supported.

Common questions

Do all partners handle every functional ingredient?

No. Ingredient suitability is evaluated individually with the manufacturing partner.

Can hemp ingredients be combined with other functional ingredients?

Submit the proposed formula and intended markets; the partner and qualified advisers can evaluate the specific proposal.

Can I use a standard 12 oz can?

Describe your intended package. Exact sizes and line availability vary.
